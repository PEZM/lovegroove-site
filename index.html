Lovegroove

Put on a record, CD or tape and let your phone listen. Lovegroove recognises the album, follows it song by song and keeps a diary of everything you play.

  • Listen once, follow the whole albumOne recognition loads the record; side changes and flips are part of the flow.
  • Your shelf, from DiscogsIt prefers the copy you own, and adds new finds to your wantlist.
  • A private listening diaryEvery play is kept on your iPhone, with stats that look like a hi-fi meter.
  • Scrobbles and favouritesOptional Last.fm, TIDAL and Apple Music, plus a lock-screen panel and Siri.
